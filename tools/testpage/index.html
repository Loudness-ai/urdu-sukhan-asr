Same decoding as the app: whisper.cpp v1.9.5, beam 5, temperature fallback, no timestamps. Runs on this server's CPU only. Times exclude model loading (shown apart), as the app keeps the model loaded. Auto-detect costs one extra encoder pass per clip (whisper.cpp encodes once just to guess the language), so a fixed language is roughly twice as fast on short clips. q4_0 is usually the fastest on x86 servers.

Record live

Speak into your microphone; when you press Stop, the recording is transcribed by the chosen models (up to 5 minutes; the browser's noise suppression and volume levelling are off, so the model hears the raw mic). The recording is deleted from the server straight after.

Your audio

Any file your browser can play (m4a, mp3, wav, ogg, video files). It is converted to 16 kHz mono in the browser, transcribed, and deleted from the server straight after.

Test clips with human transcripts

Held-out test clips (never trained on), plus no-speech clips. Shows words and letters wrong per set.
